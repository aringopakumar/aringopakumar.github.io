I'm a Computer Science student at UC Berkeley, focused on artificial intelligence and machine learning. I'm currently a Software Engineer Intern at the Department of Industrial Relations, and I spent this past summer as a Machine Learning Engineer Intern at Solugenix, where I benchmarked open-source speech-to-text and text-to-speech models to help decide which ones were worth shipping into curated, production-ready voice agents. My research interests sit broadly in machine learning, with a particular pull toward graph representation learning, sequence models, and applying machine learning to environmental and clinical problems.
